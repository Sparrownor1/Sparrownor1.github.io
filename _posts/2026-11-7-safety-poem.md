---
title: A Poem on Safety
---

Safety is found at the bottom of the breath,\
Or nestled deep in a cozy hug.

It can be a timid creature,\
And hide from the clunky footsteps\
Of those who carelessly lumber\
Around interior lodgings one tirelessly strives to maintain.

But it can also be welcomed,\
Patiently;\
And with time and care,\
continuous invitations turn fleeting visits\
Into lingering stays, until

Safety is found at the bottom of the breath.
