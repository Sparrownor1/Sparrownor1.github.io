---
title: SOSP '26 Recap
---

I had the pleasure of attending [SOSP '26](https://sigops.org/s/conferences/sosp/2026/index.html) (the Symposium on Operating Systems Principles) in Prague this September.

Some interesting things I learned about were:

- Updates on the traditional OS world
  - RAM shortage
  - Heterogenous memory architectures
  - CXL
- Esoteric systems
  - DNA Filesystems
- (Lots of) AI-centric research, especially agentic
  - YOLOFS
  - Deterministic Inference - LLM-42
  - SkillVM
  - Tons about inference and model serving
  - Shell script static analysis
- Distributed Systems wisdom
  - RPCShield
  - QProf
